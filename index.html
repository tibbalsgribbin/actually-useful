Why it exists

Amazon used to be easier to use.

I built Actually Useful because I was fed up. I usually know exactly what I want — but Amazon's search assumptions, sponsored listings, and missing filters kept getting in the way.

Actually Useful works alongside Amazon, not instead of it. It sorts and filters what's already on the page, loads more results when you want them, and puts the controls where they should have been all along.

It's free. It always will be. There are no paid features, no ads, no data sold. Just a tool that works the way you expect.

Features

What's inside the panel

Price per unit

Sorts all results by real unit price — ounces, fluid ounces, count, or whatever the category calls for. The best value item gets a star.

Keyword filter

Include terms, exclude terms, use OR logic. Mismatches dim to the bottom rather than disappear — you stay in control.

Sponsored control

Move ads to the end of results, or hide them entirely. Three states — your choice every time.

Delivery sorting

Sort by soonest delivery or soonest free delivery. Actually Useful reads the dates on each listing, not just the badge.

Shortlist

Check items as you browse, then open them all side by side in the Actually Useful comparison table — sortable, filterable, and shareable. Add notes, send the link, and the other person can filter and add their own notes and share again. Nobody else has this.

Multi-page loading

Load up to 10 pages of results and sort across all of them at once. Amazon's sort only works on one page at a time.

Source filtering

Toggle Amazon Fresh, Whole Foods, Amazon Pharmacy, and third-party retailers on or off independently.

Coupons & Subscribe and Save

Clips and Subscribe & Save discounts are surfaced and factored into sorting — not hidden until checkout.

Telemetry you control

Anonymous usage data helps fix bugs. It's on by default and off with one click in the extension popup. Nothing personal is ever sent.
