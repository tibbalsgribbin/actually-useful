Step 1 · Loading expectations

One page or seven?

Most Amazon searches load one page of results at a time. Actually Useful can load up to seven — slower to start, but you see everything before you filter. The default is four pages: enough to give the filters something real to work with, not so much that you're staring at a spinner.

A tip before the next screen: Amazon's own filters (department, brand, price range, Prime, "ships from Amazon") sit in the left sidebar of every search. The more you narrow there before letting Actually Useful expand, the more focused your loaded list becomes — and the more powerful every filter downstream gets.

You can change page count anytime in settings. The next screen lets you set it now.
